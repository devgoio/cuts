# Cutpas Privacy Policy

[简体中文](#cutpas-隐私政策)

Cutpas for macOS does not require an account. It does not use advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics, and does not send your screenshots, annotations, clipboard history, or timers to DevGo.

## Screen capture, OCR, and translation

Screen Recording permission lets Cutpas capture regions you select, including scrolling screenshots. Captures and annotations stay on your Mac unless you choose to export, copy, or share them. Text recognition and translation use macOS frameworks. macOS may separately ask to download a translation language pack. To revoke Screen Recording permission, use System Settings → Privacy & Security.

## Clipboard, timers, and diagnostics

Clipboard history reads supported text, images, and file references while Cutpas is running and keeps a bounded history on your Mac. You can delete history entries and remove favorites. Timers, associated titles and thumbnails, preferences, and a limited number of diagnostic logs are stored locally. You can delete timers and clear or turn off diagnostic logs in Cutpas Settings. Quitting Cutpas stops its clipboard monitoring. Exports are saved only where you choose.

## Support

Email [lm@usev.cn](mailto:lm@usev.cn) for support. DevGo uses your email and message to respond. You can use the same address to request deletion of your correspondence. Please do not send private screenshots or clipboard contents.

# Cutpas 隐私政策

Cutpas 不需要注册账号，不使用广告、跟踪或第三方分析工具，也不会将截图、标注、剪贴板历史或计时条目发送给 DevGo。

## 截图、文字识别和翻译

屏幕录制权限用于截取你选择的区域，包括滚动长截图。截图与标注保留在本机，除非你主动导出、复制或分享。识别与翻译使用 macOS 框架，系统可能另行提示下载翻译语言包。你可在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」撤销屏幕录制权限。

## 剪贴板、计时和诊断

运行期间，剪贴板历史读取支持的文本、图片及文件引用，并在本机保留有限数量的条目。你可以删除历史条目或取消收藏。计时、关联标题和缩略图、偏好和有限数量的诊断日志均存储于本机。你可以删除计时，并在 Cutpas 设置中清除或关闭诊断日志。退出 Cutpas 即停止剪贴板监测。导出文件保存到你选择的位置。

## 联系支持

如发邮件至 [lm@usev.cn](mailto:lm@usev.cn)，DevGo 使用邮箱地址和来信内容处理请求。可通过同一邮箱请求删除往来邮件。请勿发送私密截图或剪贴板内容。
